For details on what you need to send us to support your client's claim:

  • enter the 5-digit number from the letter received from the CRA in the box below
  • review all the information to determine what applies to your client's situation and the supporting documents required.

Note

The information on this site applies only to electronically filed returns that have been selected for review in our review programs and may be updated at any time without notice. Therefore, printing these pages is not recommended.

If you do not send us all the documents required to support your client's claim, it may be adjusted or disallowed.

Keep a copy of all the documents you sent us. We will not return photocopies or faxes unless you specifically ask us to do so. We will only return original receipts and documents once we have completed our review.

Important Notice

You are able to send us scanned documents online using Represent a Client for returns selected for review in the Processing Review Program. See Submitting documents online – Pre-assessment Review, Processing Review, Request Verification, and Matching Programs for more information.

If you received a letter from us but have not agreed to act as your client's representative for the purposes of these review programs:

  • contact the CRA and ask that the letter be sent to your client
  • verify your procedures and ensure that you used the correct contact codes for pre-assessment review and post-assessment review
