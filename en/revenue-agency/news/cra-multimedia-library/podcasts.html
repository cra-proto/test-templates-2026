Get the tax-related information you need by listening to the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) podcast.

Taxology

Welcome to the Taxology podcast! Tune in to learn more about everyone’s favourite topic: taxes! Whether you’re starting your first job, doing your first return, or need a refresher on the basics, we’ve got you! Our CRA hosts talk to experts to share tips and answer top questions. We want to you understand the basics of Canadian taxes.

Podcast episodes were recorded in an unscripted, conversational environment. That means the English and French episodes do not mirror the exact same format and content, but they have been reviewed for technical accuracy.

Episode 14 – Understanding the Collections Process

The collections process happens when you owe the CRA money. This may sound overwhelming at first, but our expert is here to guide us through the process. They’ll give you tips on how to take proactive steps to pay your debts with the CRA and how to work with a collections officer to resolve them.

Even if you or a family member don’t owe the CRA money, this episode is still a great opportunity to understand how the process works.

Episode 7: Parenting Perks: About the Canada Child Benefit

For all you parents out there!

Maybe you have a newborn, adopted a child, or have a new parenting situation. If you have a new child in your life, it’s important to let the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) know since it can affect your taxes and benefits, like the Canada child benefit (CCB)

On this episode of Taxology, a CRA expert explains the CCB, clarifies misconceptions and shares how you can apply.

We can’t help you change diapers or build a crib, but we can help you get the benefits and credits you’re eligible for! So get that baby carrier on, and we will talk while you walk.

Episode 6: Explaining the Disability Tax Credit

The disability tax credit (DTC) is a non-refundable credit that helps persons with disabilities who have a severe and prolonged physical or mental impairment affecting their daily lives.

In this episode, we chat with a CRA expert on the DTC, to help you understand the credit and the application process, and to clear up any misconceptions.
